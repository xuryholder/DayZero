# DayZero

> Your startup failed. Your story didn’t.

Built during the **SEABW 2026 Play to Build: AI Hackathon** in Bangkok — a 24-hour challenge focused on solving real-world problems using AI.

DayZero addresses one of the most ignored problems in tech and Web3:
what happens to founders after failure.

An anonymous recovery and rebuilding network for founders after startup collapse, burnout, shutdowns, pivots, layoffs, or emotional exhaustion.

---

# Vision

Most startup ecosystems celebrate success.

Almost nobody helps founders survive failure.

DayZero exists to change that.

We believe:
- failure is part of mastery
- collapsed startups still create experienced founders
- rebuilding should be social, not lonely
- founders deserve a second chapter

---

# Core Philosophy

> Failure is not exile from the ecosystem.

DayZero transforms:
- shame → experience
- collapse → learning
- burnout → recovery
- isolation → community
- endings → new beginnings

# MVP Features

## Anonymous Founder Profiles

Safe identity system for founders who want:
- privacy
- emotional safety
- honest discussions
- reputation protection

Optional founder verification:
- LinkedIn
- GitHub
- Twitter/X
- startup proof
- wallet history

---

## Founder Stories

Raw startup postmortems:
- shutdown stories
- cofounder conflicts
- fundraising failures
- burnout experiences
- pivots
- lessons learned

Examples:
- “We raised $2M and died in 9 months”
- “My cofounder disappeared before launch”
- “After shutdown I couldn’t code for 6 months”

---

## Recovery Circles

Private founder groups focused on:
- burnout
- startup collapse
- investor pressure
- rebuilding confidence
- emotional recovery

Small trusted groups:
- 5–10 founders
- weekly sessions
- optional anonymity

---

## Startup Autopsy AI

AI-powered startup failure analysis.

Users upload:
- pitch decks
- metrics
- tokenomics
- roadmaps
- product data

AI analyzes:
- market timing
- burn rate
- founder conflict
- distribution problems
- PMF issues
- tokenomics mistakes
- operational risks

Goal:
help founders learn without self-destruction.

---

## Rebuild Network

Second-chance ecosystem for:
- jobs
- cofounders
- advisors
- investors
- operators

Focused on:
> experienced founders rebuilding after failure.

---

## Founder Journal

Private emotional recovery journal.

AI can help track:
- stress
- burnout
- emotional patterns
- recovery progress
- founder resilience
